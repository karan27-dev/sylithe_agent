# Maintenance Bulletin MB-2026-17 REV 2

**Subject:** Interim monitoring for TK-4102
**Issued:** 15-Sep-2026 · Inspection Department
**Supersedes:** MB-2026-17 (13-Sep-2026)

## Background

Routine UT survey on 12-Sep-2026 recorded shell plate thickness of
**11.2 mm** at course-1 against a nominal of 12.0 mm. The minimum required
thickness has been recalculated per API 653 as **10.2 mm** (previous
bulletin stated 10.4 mm; the corrosion allowance figure used was corrected).

## Required Actions

| # | Action | Owner | Due |
|---|--------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Quarterly UT at course-1 (4 points) | Inspection | 31-Dec-2026 |
| 2 | Bench test and reset PSV-2041 to 14.0 barg | Maintenance | 15-Oct-2026 |
| 3 | Visual check of nozzle N-3 each shift | Operations | Ongoing |
| 4 | Verify calibration of level transmitter LT-4102 | Instrumentation | 01-Oct-2026 |

## Notes

- Operating temperature must not exceed **60 degC** while MOC-2026-0231 is
  in force (revised down from 65 degC).
- Any reading below 10.8 mm shall be escalated to the Head of Inspection
  the same day.

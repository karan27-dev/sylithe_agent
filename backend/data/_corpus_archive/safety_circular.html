Safety Circular SC-2026-09

To: All CDU-II shift engineers
Date: 13-Sep-2026

Relief valve settings

A review of relief devices in CDU-II found PSV-2041 set at 12.5 barg, whereas SOP-114 clause 4 requires 14.0 barg for atmospheric storage service. This is recorded as non-conformance NCR-2026-0088.

Immediate instruction

  1. Do not raise TK-4102 operating level above 82% until the valve is reset.
  2. Log tank level hourly in the shift register.
  3. Report any relief device lift to the Control Room immediately.

Issued by: A. Deshpande, Head — Inspection

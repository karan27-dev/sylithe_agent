# MRPL Inspection Department - Interval and Acceptance Policy

Document No. MRPL-INSP-POL-009 | Revision 4 | Effective 01-Feb-2026

## 1. Corrosion rate

The long-term corrosion rate shall be calculated as the difference between the
earliest and the most recent governing thickness readings at the same location,
divided by the elapsed interval in years.

    corrosion rate (mm/yr) = (t_earlier - t_recent) / interval_years

## 2. Remaining life

    remaining life (years) = (t_recent - t_min) / corrosion rate

## 3. Next inspection interval

Per API 510, the next internal or on-stream inspection interval shall be the
lesser of one half of the remaining life, or ten years.

## 4. Relief device acceptance

Unless a more restrictive vendor or process requirement applies, the as-found
popping pressure of a spring loaded relief device shall be within plus or minus
3 percent of the nameplate set pressure, per API 527.

The acceptance band shall be written out explicitly before a conformance
judgement is recorded. A device whose as-found value lies inside the band is
conforming, and shall not be withdrawn from service on thickness or set
pressure grounds alone.

## 5. Deviations

A deviation approval applies only to the equipment tag named in it, and only
until its stated expiry date. A deviation shall never be applied by analogy to
similar equipment.

## 6. Superseded documents

Where two revisions of a procedure exist, only the revision marked CURRENT is
valid for field execution. A superseded revision shall be named as superseded
if it is referenced at all.

# Unit 33 inspection policy - extract

## 3. Corrosion rate

The corrosion rate shall be calculated at the **governing CML**, which is the **thinnest** location, using the earliest and the most recent survey. Averaging CMLs is not permitted.

    rate = (t_earlier - t_recent) / interval_years

## 4. Remaining life

Remaining life shall be calculated from the **most recent measured thickness**, never from nominal:

    remaining life = (t_recent - t_min) / rate

## 5. Next inspection interval

The next inspection interval shall be the **lesser of half the remaining life, or 10 years**.

## 6. Relief device acceptance

As-found set pressure shall lie within **+/- 3% of the nameplate set pressure**. The band shall be written out in full before any verdict is recorded. A device outside the band is NON-CONFORMING unless an approved deviation is in force for that specific device.

## 7. Deviations

A deviation applies only to the equipment named in it. A deviation shall never be applied by analogy to similar equipment.
